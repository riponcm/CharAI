# Code of conduct

CharAI is a place to make people move. We want everyone who shows up, of any age, background or experience, to feel able to ask, try, get it wrong and try again.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and receive it without taking it as an attack.
- Respect other people's time and privacy. Do not share anything personal about someone without their say.
- Credit other people's work. Share only what you made or have the right to share.

## What we will not accept

Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, sexual or demeaning content, doxxing, spam, and characters or films made to mock or harm real people.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and files that break these rules, and may block someone who keeps breaking them. We will try to explain why.

## Reporting

To tell us about a problem privately, use GitHub's report feature on the repository (the "Report content" option on a comment, or the report link on the repository). Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private as far as we can.

This applies in the repository, its issues and discussions, and anywhere else you speak for the project.
