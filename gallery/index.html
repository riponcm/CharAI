gallery

Little films about people, made with , a 2D character animation engine. Every clip below is a short text script: no frames were drawn by hand. The engine draws the characters only; the plain backdrop comes from the render tool or from this page.

Clips

Six clips at 1280x720. Press play, or open the gif for a quick look.

Six very different people in one line-up

A toddler, a grandfather, a baker, a tall woman, an athlete and a teenager, each drawn from a few numbers and paths, all on one stage.

The toddler and the captain

A small child runs to an old sea captain, trips, cries, is comforted and plays peekaboo. Camera cuts and zooms are part of the script.

The talking scene, with sound

Two characters talk in close-up. The voices are the built-in Windows voices; the lip sync follows their timing. This clip has sound.

The hug, the handover and the scuffle

Characters that touch: a child hugs the baker and gets a loaf, two friends embrace, and a playful fight breaks out.

Four moods, one walk

The same walk in happy, sad, angry and scared, on three different bodies. One mood line changes everything.

Ten actions, three bodies

A montage of catalogue actions: jump for joy, salute, think, bow, clap, shrug, stomp, tantrum, yawn, jump. Each action is one small file of key poses.
